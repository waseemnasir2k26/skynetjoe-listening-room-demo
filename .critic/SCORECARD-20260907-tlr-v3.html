Majors

  • MJ1Form confirmation is silent to assistive tech. On submit the form is swapped for the .ok panel with no role="status", no aria-live, and no focus move — the file contains zero aria-live / role="status" / role="alert" occurrences, and the sole .focus() call (line 2456) is the validation-error path. A screen-reader user gets no signal that the request went through. WCAG 2.2 AA 4.1.3 (Status Messages). Fix before the client build: add role="status" to .ok and move focus to its heading.

Why it earns distinctiveness

The ear motif is carried as a real system — header mark, hero call pill, contact-panel medallion — rather than decorating one hero. The serif italic accent on "Not managed." does the whole emotional argument in two words. The crisis block inverts to white and refuses every animation on the page, which reads as a deliberate tonal break rather than a stray section. Green/black/white is held with enough restraint that the one green the eye lands on is always the next action.
